--- Technical (HN / r/MachineLearning) ---

Solar irradiance nowcasting for Singapore, 1-3h ahead.

Architecture: one EfficientNet-B0 encoder, frozen and weight-tied across three
satellite frames + an RoI crop, cross-attention fusion onto a BiLSTM over 24h
of weather. 4.8M params, 1.24M trainable — deliberately small for ~5k samples.

Target is the clear-sky index k_t rather than raw GHI, so the loss isn't
dominated by the diurnal cycle, with the NLL weighted by future clear-sky.
Checkpoints selected on validation MAE, not NLL, because MAE is what's reported.

Held-out test (n=1015, never used for training or selection):
  v3 ensemble (3 seeds, precision-weighted)  63.38
  LightGBM                                   66.23
  Random Forest                              66.34
  smart persistence                          86.01

Diebold-Mariano on the loss difference: Δ −2.84 W/m², DM −2.05, p = 0.0402,
HAC lags 15. All three seeds beat LightGBM individually.

Caveats I'd want to see if someone else posted this:
  - p = 0.0402 is marginal. On synthetic AR(1) nulls at this n the test fired
    at ~8% against a nominal 5%, so read it as weak evidence, not settled.
  - The target is ERA5 shortwave radiation, not a pyranometer, and the tabular
    features come from the same ERA5 call. Not comparable to literature that
    validates against ground measurements.
  - Numbers are from an Apple-silicon run. The same v2 checkpoints score 11-19
    W/m² apart between this machine and RunPod for reasons not yet found.
